MESSAGE 9 — 6-Step Operating Model [FROM SUMMARY]
================================================================================

The 6-step operating model for the DWA agent, refined over multiple messages:

1. Gather account + scenario from the user
2. Research account externally via web search
3. Review Industry Solutions & Value Engineering materials
4. Apply positioning angle based on client's AI strategy
   (two scenarios: client HAS AI platform vs does NOT)
5. Build differentiating position around core pillars
6. Produce PowerPoint deck from corporate template or
   executive-quality chat briefing

- All output must be value-led (what the customer achieves, not product features)
- Value-led positioning framework: L3 = Labor, Logistics, Liability ROI
- Combine all sources in output
