SECTION 1 — APP CONCEPT & ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

Build a web application called "Tungsten Automation AI Positioning."

PURPOSE:
A content generator agent for Tungsten Automation's global sales team. Account
Executives, BDRs, and Sales Engineers use it to generate account-specific,
value-led sales positioning content — both as interactive AI chat AND as
downloadable PowerPoint decks and Word documents.

TECHNOLOGY STACK:
- Next.js 16 (app directory, TypeScript)
- React 19
- Tailwind CSS 4
- Vercel AI SDK (ai v6, @ai-sdk/react v3, @ai-sdk/anthropic v3)
- Claude claude-opus-4-6 model for all AI interactions
- Python 3 + python-pptx + python-docx for file generation
- Port: 3002 (dev and production)

AI MODEL: Claude claude-opus-4-6
- Chat streaming: max 4096 output tokens, 120s timeout
- Deck/doc generation: max 16000 output tokens, 300s timeout

This is a FOUNDATIONAL POSITIONING PRINCIPLE that must underpin every
interaction. Woven into all positioning even when not explicitly raised.

THE MARKET REALITY (cite these stats):
- 80-90% of enterprise data is unstructured
- 95% of in-house GenAI pilots fail to deliver measurable value (MIT 2025)
- 42% of companies abandoned most AI initiatives in 2025 (S&P Global)
- Only 26% of AI projects succeed beyond proof of concept (BCG)
- Vendor solutions succeed at 2x the rate of DIY builds (67% vs 33%)
- Top obstacles: data quality/readiness (43%), technical maturity (43%),
  skills shortage (35%)
- Winning AI programmes earmark 50-70% of timeline and budget for data
  readiness — this is exactly what Tungsten solves

THE ACCURACY FALLACY:
Foundation models are commoditised — same LLMs used by everyone. What
differentiates real-world accuracy is OPERATIONALISATION: feedback loops,
human-in-the-loop, continuous learning, governance. Lab accuracy ≠ production
accuracy. 95% of GenAI pilots fail when built internally.

- Engineering time: 6-12 months to MVP. To match TotalAgility with Microsoft
  tools alone: 5-6 separate products + 6+ months
- Technical debt: compounds year after year
- Compliance & governance: audit trails, data residency, PII redaction,
  GDPR/HIPAA/PCI all custom-built
- AI talent scarcity: expensive, in short supply, pilots stall before production
- ELA shelfware risk: capacity ≠ business value

THE LEGO PROBLEM:
Building from hyperscaler tools means stitching together OCR engines,
classification models, extraction pipelines, validation rules, workflow
orchestration, human review interfaces, audit logging, monitoring.
"Every join is a point of slippage where data falls out, accuracy degrades,
or traceability is lost."
"Who maintains it? What audit trail exists? Can you track documents end-to-end?"

- Continuous learning from corrections — prevents drift and hallucinations
- BYOLLM: OpenAI, Microsoft, Anthropic, Google, Llama, Mistral, custom — NO LOCK-IN

- Technical debt: ELIMINATED — vendor-managed upgrades
